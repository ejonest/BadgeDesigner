How It Works
Designing and ordering your custom name badges is simple — no design experience needed, no back-and-forth with a salesperson, no artwork fees.
________________


Step 1 — Design It Live
Use our custom badge tool to build your badge in real time. Choose your shape, size, background color, and material. Type in your name, title, or any text you want — and watch your badge come to life on screen before you commit to anything.
No guesswork. No waiting on proofs. What you see is exactly what you get.
________________


Step 2 — Pick Your Quantity
Badges are priced by volume — the more you order, the less you pay per badge. Whether you need 1 badge for a new hire or 500 for a full team rollout, our pricing scales with you. See exactly what you'll pay before you check out.
________________


Step 3 — We Make Your Badges & Ship
Once your order is placed, our team gets to work. Most custom badge orders ship within 2 business days. We engrave or print every badge in-house in our US facility using professional laser engraving equipment or UV Printing — no outsourcing, no delays.
________________


Step 4 — Reorder Anytime in One Click
Your designs are saved to your account. When a new hire joins or you need a replacement badge, just pull up your saved design and reorder — no starting from scratch.
